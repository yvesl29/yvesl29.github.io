## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's store:

- **iOS**: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Darts
- **Android**: Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions > Darts

### How do I delete my data?

Send us an email at [contact@ondeinteractive.com](mailto:contact@ondeinteractive.com) requesting data deletion. To help us locate your data, please include:

- **Analytics identifier**: Found in the app under Settings > Privacy
- **Subscription identifier**: Found in the app under Settings > Privacy

We will process your request within 30 days.

### How do I request my data?

Under GDPR, you can request a copy of your data. Email us at [contact@ondeinteractive.com](mailto:contact@ondeinteractive.com) and we will provide your data in a machine-readable format within 30 days.
