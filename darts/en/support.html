Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's store:

  • iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Darts
  • Android: Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions > Darts

How do I delete my data?

Send us an email at contact@ondeinteractive.com requesting data deletion. We will process your request within 30 days.

How do I request my data?

Under GDPR, you can request a copy of your data. Email us at contact@ondeinteractive.com and we will provide your data in a machine-readable format within 30 days.
